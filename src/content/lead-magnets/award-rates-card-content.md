## Important Notes

1. **Classification matters.** An electrician completing complex or supervisory work is likely Grade 3–5, not Grade 1. Misclassification is the #1 underpayment trigger in FWO audits of trades businesses.

2. **Apprentice percentages are applied to Grade 1 — not the grade of the supervising tradesperson.** Common error: applying Year 3 apprentice % to Grade 3 supervisor's rate. Wrong.

3. **Tool allowance applies even when tools are only partly supplied by the employee.** If they bring any tools, the allowance applies — check the award clause.

4. **Industry allowance is not optional.** It applies to all employees performing on-site electrical installation work, regardless of business size.

5. **Backpay exposure is 6 years** under the Fair Work Act. A $2/hr underpayment over 6 years = ~$25,000 per employee.
