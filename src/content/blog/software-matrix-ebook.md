## The Four Contenders

For Australian tradies in 2026, the market has four serious contenders:

- **ServiceM8** — the market leader for Australian small-to-mid trade businesses, strong compliance support, unlimited users
- **SimPRO** — enterprise-grade, built for 10+ staff businesses, powerful job costing, complex setup

Each platform has strengths. Each has trade-offs. And each has hidden costs that aren't obvious from the pricing page.
