AroFlo is an Australian-built field service management platform used by trade businesses across electrical, HVAC, plumbing, and fire protection. It's positioned as a comprehensive alternative to simPRO for businesses that need project management and asset management without simPRO's enterprise scale and price.

This review gives you an honest assessment of what AroFlo does well, where it falls short, and whether it's the right fit for your electrical or solar business.

---

## What AroFlo Is Built For

AroFlo's design centres on two capabilities that distinguish it from simpler platforms:

**Asset and maintenance management** — AroFlo builds a register of client assets (switchboards, exit lights, air conditioning units, solar inverters) and attaches service history, compliance certificates, and scheduled maintenance to each asset. For businesses running ongoing maintenance contracts, this is genuinely valuable — automated reminders, renewal tracking, and compliance certificate management all flow from the asset register.

**Multi-phase project management** — Commercial projects with multiple stages, progress billing, and detailed cost tracking are handled through AroFlo's project module. It's not as deep as simPRO on estimating, but covers most commercial project management needs.

**Compliance documentation** — AroFlo has a forms builder and compliance certificate management that can handle state electrical certificates, test reports, and audit documentation. It's configurable rather than pre-built for specific compliance types.

## What AroFlo Does Well

### Asset management and maintenance scheduling

This is AroFlo's standout capability. The asset register links client sites, individual assets, their service history, and due-date scheduling into one view. For a business with 50 clients on annual exit lighting contracts or monthly switchboard maintenance, this structure saves significant admin time and prevents maintenance falling through the cracks.

### Compliance certificate management

AroFlo's compliance module lets you attach test reports and compliance certificates to assets. For businesses generating significant compliance paperwork (test and tag, RCD testing, AS/NZS 3000 inspections), the ability to track certificate status by asset and client is useful.

### Job costing

AroFlo tracks material cost, labour cost, and margin at the job level. Not as deep as simPRO on complex commercial estimating, but adequate for most project and maintenance work.

### Client portal

AroFlo includes a client portal where customers can view job history, compliance certificates, and asset registers. For commercial clients with multiple sites, this is valuable — they get self-service access to their service records.

### Australian-built support

Being Australian-built and supported means AroFlo's team understands local compliance requirements, award rates, and trade business practices. Support quality is generally rated positively by users.

---

## Where AroFlo Falls Short

### No pre-built CCEW form

AroFlo doesn't have a pre-built CCEW (Certificate of Compliance for Electrical Work) for NSW electricians. You can configure compliance forms through the form builder, but it requires setup during implementation. ServiceM8's CCEW is ready to use out of the box.

### Cost versus simpler alternatives

For businesses that don't need the asset management depth, AroFlo's per-user pricing is hard to justify versus ServiceM8. A 5-person team at $400–$750/month vs ServiceM8 at $199/month is a $200–$550/month difference — $2,400–$6,600/year.

### Implementation time

AroFlo's implementation typically takes 4–8 weeks for a mid-sized business. If you want to be operational quickly, this timeline is a barrier.

### Mobile app

AroFlo's mobile app handles the basics — job viewing, time entry, photos, forms — but is generally considered less field-friendly than ServiceM8. The interface reflects the system's complexity.

## Who Should Use AroFlo

**Good fit:**
- Trade businesses with significant ongoing maintenance contract work (exit lighting, RCD testing, air conditioning service, solar inverter maintenance)
- 8–30 staff operations that need asset registers and compliance certificate tracking
- Businesses moving off paper-based maintenance systems who need systematic scheduling
- Electrical, HVAC, or fire protection businesses with multi-site commercial clients

**Not a good fit:**
- Businesses primarily doing residential installation work with minimal maintenance contracts
- Small teams (1–6 staff) where the cost and implementation time is disproportionate
- NSW electrical businesses that need a plug-and-play CCEW workflow
- Businesses that want to be up and running in days rather than weeks

---

## The Verdict

AroFlo fills a specific niche well: Australian trade businesses with ongoing maintenance contracts that need proper asset management, compliance tracking, and scheduled service automation. In that use case, the cost and implementation investment can be justified.

For most electrical and solar installation businesses — where the work is project-based rather than maintenance-contract-heavy — AroFlo's cost is harder to justify versus ServiceM8. The asset management depth you're paying for isn't what your business primarily needs.

If you're evaluating AroFlo, ask yourself: do you currently have 20+ assets under ongoing maintenance contracts? If yes, AroFlo's structure is likely worth the investment. If your work is primarily installation and project-based, ServiceM8 handles your workflow at a fraction of the cost.
