Solar and battery system pricing in Australia has changed significantly over the last three years. Component costs have shifted — panel prices dropped sharply in 2023–24 before stabilising, while battery prices have continued to fall. Labour costs have increased. Installer margins have compressed on commoditised residential solar while strengthening on battery and complex installations.

For solar installers, understanding the current cost landscape matters for two reasons: accurate, defensible quoting that protects your margin, and customer education that builds trust and closes sales. Customers who understand what drives cost are easier to close — and less likely to walk away to the cheapest competitor.

This guide covers realistic solar and battery system costs in Australia in 2026, what's included in a proper installation, how rebates reduce customer cost, and how to use pricing transparency as a sales tool.

## Solar Panel System Costs in 2026

Solar panel system pricing varies significantly based on system size, component quality, location, and installation complexity. The following ranges reflect typical installed prices across Australian metropolitan and near-metro areas for standard residential installations.

### Residential Solar System Pricing

| System Size | Quality Tier | Installed Price (Before STC) | STC Value (approx.) | Customer Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6.6kW | Mid-range | $5,500–$7,500 | $1,800–$2,400 | $3,100–$5,700 |
| 6.6kW | Premium | $7,500–$10,000 | $1,800–$2,400 | $5,100–$8,200 |
| 10kW | Mid-range | $8,000–$11,000 | $2,700–$3,600 | $5,300–$8,400 |
| 10kW | Premium | $10,000–$14,000 | $2,700–$3,600 | $7,300–$11,400 |
| 13kW | Mid-range | $10,000–$14,000 | $3,500–$4,700 | $6,500–$10,500 |
| 13kW | Premium | $13,000–$18,000 | $3,500–$4,700 | $9,500–$14,300 |

*STC values are approximate based on Zone 3 (Brisbane, Sydney) and mid-2026 market prices. Zone 1 (Darwin) is higher; Zone 4 (Melbourne) and Zone 5 (Hobart, Canberra) are lower. Use the CER's STC calculator for precise values.*

### What's Included in a Quality Solar Installation

A properly quoted solar installation includes:

- **Solar panels** (and panel warranties — 25+ years product, 25+ years performance)
- **Inverter** (string inverter, microinverters, or string + optimisers — each has cost and performance implications)
- **Mounting system** (roof-specific racking — tilt frame, flush, standing seam — appropriate for the roof type)
- **DC and AC cabling** (including conduit where required)
- **Isolators** (DC at the array, AC at the inverter and switchboard)
- **Switchboard modification** (where required)
- **Network connection application** (where required by the DNSP)
- **CEC-accredited design and installation**
- **STC creation and assignment** (deducted from the customer's invoice)
- **Compliance certification** (state-specific electrical certificate)
- **Grid connection monitoring setup** (app access, performance monitoring)

Quotes that exclude any of these components are not comparable on price. If you're competing against a quote that's $2,000 cheaper, find out what's been excluded before discounting.

## Battery Storage System Costs in 2026

Battery prices have continued to fall in 2025–26. Entry-level battery storage (5–10kWh) is now within reach for a broader range of Australian households.

### Residential Battery System Pricing

| Battery System | Usable Capacity | Installed Price (Before Rebates) |
|---|---|---|
| Entry-level (e.g., BYD Battery-Box, Sungrow SBH) | 5–7kWh | $6,000–$9,000 |
| Mid-range (e.g., Enphase IQ Battery, LGES RESU) | 8–12kWh | $9,000–$14,000 |
| Premium (e.g., Tesla Powerwall 3, SolarEdge Home) | 10–16kWh | $12,000–$18,000 |
| Larger storage (stacked battery systems) | 20kWh+ | $20,000–$35,000+ |

*Prices include supply, installation, switchboard work, and compliance certificate. Battery prices have decreased significantly from 2022–23 levels and may continue to fall — verify current pricing with your distributors.*

### What Drives Battery Cost Variation

**Brand and chemistry:** Lithium iron phosphate (LFP) batteries (BYD, SolarEdge, Sungrow) are typically less expensive per kWh than NMC chemistry batteries. LFP also has better safety characteristics and longer cycle life for most residential use cases.

**System integration:** AC-coupled batteries (Tesla Powerwall, LGES RESU) can be retrofitted to any existing solar system regardless of inverter brand. DC-coupled batteries (SolarEdge, Enphase, SMA Sunny Boy Storage) are more efficient but require a compatible inverter — sometimes requiring an inverter replacement.

**Installation complexity:** Multi-storey homes, difficult switchboard locations, and long cable runs increase installation cost. A basement switchboard in a three-storey home is a materially different installation from a single-storey slab house.

## How Rebates Reduce Customer Cost

Rebates and incentives significantly reduce the customer's out-of-pocket cost. As the installer, understanding and clearly communicating the full incentive picture is a direct sales advantage.

### Federal: STCs for Solar Panels

STCs reduce the customer's upfront solar cost. They're created based on system size and location, assigned to the retailer/installer at point of sale, and deducted from the customer's invoice as an upfront discount.

The [STC claim process guide](/blog/stc-claim-process-solar-installers-australia) covers how STCs are calculated, created, and assigned — and what compliance requirements must be met for every STC-eligible installation.

### State Battery Rebates

State governments offer battery rebates across multiple jurisdictions in 2026:

- **Victoria:** Solar Homes Program — up to $2,950 rebate + up to $8,800 interest-free loan. See the [Solar Victoria rebate guide](/blog/vic-solar-homes-program-rebate-2026).
- **South Australia:** Home Battery Scheme — subsidised loan up to $10,000. See the [SA battery rebate guide](/blog/sa-solar-battery-rebate-2026).
- **QLD, WA, NSW:** Various state programs — verify current availability directly with each state government.

### Stacking Incentives

A Victorian household installing 10kW solar + 13.5kWh battery can access:
- STCs for the solar component: ~$2,700–$3,600
- Solar Victoria battery rebate: up to $2,950
- Solar Victoria interest-free loan: up to $8,800

Total incentive value: potentially $14,000–$15,000+ in combined rebates and interest-free financing against a gross installed cost of $18,000–$25,000. The customer's out-of-pocket can be as low as $3,000–$10,000 depending on loan uptake and current rebate availability.

Presenting the stacked incentive picture confidently and clearly is one of the highest-leverage things you can do in a solar + battery sales conversation.

## What Drives Cost Variation Between Quotes

Customers who get three solar quotes are often confused by price differences of $2,000–$5,000 for what appears to be the same system size. Help them understand the real drivers:

**Panel quality and brand:** A Tier 1 panel with a 25-year product warranty from a manufacturer with a legitimate Australian distribution network is not the same as a commodity panel from a brand with no local service presence. Panel degradation rates, hot-spot performance, and warranty claim processes differ materially.

**Inverter quality and warranty:** A 10-year inverter warranty from a manufacturer with Australian service infrastructure is not the same as a 5-year warranty from a brand that has no local repair centre. Inverter failures are the most common solar system fault — and the warranty service experience matters when one occurs.

**Installer reputation and compliance:** A CEC-accredited installer with documented compliance processes, proper CCEW/CES lodgement, and a digital paper trail is not the same as an installer who completes the electrical work but treats paperwork as optional. If the compliance isn't done, the installation isn't eligible for STCs and may not be covered by the customer's home insurance.

**Post-installation support:** Monitoring setup, fault response, and system performance guarantees vary widely. The cheapest installer at the time of sale may be the most expensive over the 25-year system life.
