## The STC You Didn't Claim Is Safer Than the One You Claimed Wrong

Here's the asymmetry that catches solar installers off guard:

The STCs you **don't** claim — because of conservative system sizing, missed eligible panels, or overlooked component upgrades — never create a compliance problem. You just leave money on the table.

The STCs you **do** claim carry a permanent documentation obligation. If the CER asks for proof and your file doesn't have the geo-tagged photo, the serial number record, or the compliance certificate, that STC becomes a liability — not an asset.

The e-book is built around closing both gaps: claiming **every** STC you're entitled to, but claiming them in a way that passes audit every time.
