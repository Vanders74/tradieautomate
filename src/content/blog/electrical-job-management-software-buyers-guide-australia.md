Australian electrical contractors lose an average of $12,000+ per year to administrative inefficiency — unbilled hours, delayed invoicing, lost job records, and manual compliance paperwork. This is compounded by the compliance obligations electrical contractors face: under the **Home Building Act 1989**, **NSW Fair Trading** requires a Certificate of Compliance for Electrical Work (CCEW) for all notifiable electrical work, and the **Clean Energy Regulator** administers STC eligibility under the **Renewable Energy (Electricity) Act 2000** for solar-related installations. Job management software exists to recover that time and money while keeping your compliance audit-ready.

This guide cuts through the platform noise and gives you a clear framework for choosing the right software for your electrical contracting business.

---

## Why Electrical Contractors Need Specialised Software

Generic invoicing tools (Xero alone, MYOB invoicing, even Excel) don't handle the operational reality of an electrical contracting business:

- **CCEW documentation** must be completed, signed, and retained for every job in NSW — this is a regulatory requirement, not optional
- **Multi-crew scheduling** across simultaneous jobs requires real-time visibility
- **Mobile invoicing** from the site, not from an office three days later
- **Quote-to-job workflow** that doesn't require re-entering information
- **Photo and document attachment** to jobs for warranty and compliance evidence

A proper job management system handles all of these as integrated workflow, not disconnected tools.

## The Platforms — Full Comparison

### ServiceM8

**Best for:** 1–15 staff, residential and light commercial electrical

ServiceM8 was built for mobile-first trade businesses and is the market leader for small-to-medium Australian electrical contractors.

**Pricing:** $29–$349/month (no per-user fees; priced on monthly job volume)

**Electrical-specific features:**
- Pre-built CCEW form (NSW electrical compliance certificate) — completes, signs, and stores in the job record
- Custom form builder for state-specific compliance (VIC Certificate of Electrical Safety, SA Electrical Certificate of Compliance, etc.)
- GPS job scheduling and real-time staff location
- "On My Way" customer notification with ETA
- Client portal — customers access job history and documents
- Xero/MYOB/QuickBooks integration at no extra cost

**What sets it apart:**
The CCEW form integration is genuinely differentiated. For NSW electrical businesses, the compliance workflow is built into the job — the electrician completes the CCEW digitally on their phone, the customer signs on-screen, and the certificate is automatically emailed. No separate paperwork.

**Limitations:** Multi-day complex commercial project management is functional but not as deep as simPRO or AroFlo.

---

### simPRO

**Best for:** 10+ staff, complex commercial projects, larger operations

simPRO is the enterprise-tier option for Australian trade businesses. It's more powerful than ServiceM8 but significantly more expensive and complex to implement.

**Pricing:** ~$150–$250/user/month (varies by contract and modules)

**Electrical-specific strengths:**
- Advanced estimating for large commercial electrical projects
- Multi-phase project management and progress tracking
- Detailed job costing and margin reporting
- CEC documentation support for solar electrical work
- Asset management for ongoing maintenance contracts

**The honest verdict:** For electrical businesses with 3–8 staff doing mostly residential and small commercial work, simPRO is overkill. The cost is 3–5× ServiceM8 for a small team, and the implementation takes weeks. Worth it when: you're doing consistent large commercial projects, you need detailed multi-phase project tracking, and you have dedicated admin staff to manage the system.

---

### Tradify

**Best for:** 1–5 staff, simple workflow, budget-conscious operators

Tradify is the simplest of the mainstream trade job management platforms. It handles quotes, jobs, invoicing, and scheduling cleanly.

**Pricing:** ~$35/user/month

**For electricians:**
Tradify lacks a native CCEW form. You can attach PDF documents to jobs, but there's no structured digital compliance form completion. This is manageable for small operations but is a real limitation versus ServiceM8 for NSW electrical businesses with regular CCEW requirements.

**The honest verdict:** A legitimate option for solo operators or small teams who prioritise simplicity and want to spend less time learning software. Clear upgrade path to ServiceM8 when complexity increases.

---

### AroFlo

**Best for:** Larger trade businesses with complex project and asset management needs

AroFlo is a comprehensive, powerful platform with strong project management and asset management capabilities. Pricing is custom and typically higher than simPRO.

For most electrical businesses under 15 staff, AroFlo is expensive and complex relative to the benefit. Relevant for: large electrical contractors doing multi-site maintenance contracts, asset-heavy commercial operations, or businesses needing custom ERP-style integration.

---

### Fergus

**Best for:** Small-medium trade businesses wanting a simpler alternative to simPRO

Fergus sits between Tradify and simPRO in sophistication and cost (~$49/user/month). It handles quoting, scheduling, job management, and invoicing well.

For electricians specifically: limited native compliance documentation tools. The workflow is strong but the CCEW gap is real for NSW businesses.

## Decision Framework

**Choose ServiceM8 if:**
- You're a 1–15 person electrical business
- You do residential and light commercial work
- CCEW compliance is a regular requirement
- You want fast setup and a mobile-first system
- You're on Xero or MYOB and want seamless integration

**Choose simPRO if:**
- You have 10+ field staff
- You regularly manage large commercial electrical projects
- You need detailed multi-phase project management and job costing
- You have admin staff to manage the system
- Budget is not the primary constraint

**Choose Tradify if:**
- You're a solo operator or have 1–2 staff
- Simplicity is the priority over depth
- CCEW is not a daily requirement (or you can manage it separately)
- You're cost-sensitive at the early stage

## Implementation Checklist

> **When getting started with your chosen platform:**
>
> 1. **Import your client database first** — this takes the most time and is foundational.
> 2. **Set up your services and pricing** before the first job — avoid quoting from memory.
> 3. **Build your compliance forms** (CCEW, CES, or state equivalent) in week one — this is the highest-value workflow change.
> 4. **Connect accounting software** before the first invoice goes out.
> 5. **Train field staff on mobile** before they go on their first job — 30 minutes of training is all most platforms need.
