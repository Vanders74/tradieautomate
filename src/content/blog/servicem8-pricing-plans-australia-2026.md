## Hidden Costs — What ServiceM8 Doesn't Highlight

### Cost #1: The Job Cap Trap

ServiceM8's job limits are hard caps on Starter, Growing, and Premium plans — not soft limits. If you hit 150 jobs mid-month on Growing, you cannot create more jobs until you upgrade. This is different from competitors like Tradify and Fergus, which have unlimited jobs on all paid plans.

**The real risk:** Seasonal businesses. A solar installer doing 40 jobs/month most of the year but 80 jobs/month in summer peak will outgrow Starter (50 jobs) during peak months. You either upgrade permanently or accept the disruption of mid-month plan changes.

**Mitigation:** ServiceM8 makes upgrading instant and prorated. If you hit your cap on day 20, upgrade to the next tier — you'll only pay the difference for the remaining days. The alternative (downgrading) takes effect at the next billing cycle.

### Cost #2: SMS Overage

SMS is the hidden cost that catches most new ServiceM8 users. Every automated client notification — booking confirmation, "On the Way" text, job reminder, invoice SMS — burns through your allowance at 1 message each.

**Real example:** A 5-person crew doing 120 jobs/month sends approximately:
- 120 booking confirmations
- 120 "On the Way" texts
- 100 invoice/follow-up SMS
- Total: ~340 SMS/month

On the Growing plan (300 SMS allowance), that's 40 SMS over at 10c each = $4/month. Negligible. But on Starter (100 SMS), the same business would exceed by 240 SMS = $24/month — nearly doubling the plan cost.

**Rule of thumb:** Budget 2-3 SMS per job. If your plan allowance is less than 2.5× your job volume, you'll pay overage.

### Cost #3: ServiceM8 Pay Transaction Fees Are Real Money

On $60,000/month invoiced through card payments, Stripe fees at 1.75% + 30c come to ~$1,050/month. That's real money — but it replaces 21-28 day payment terms with same-day settlement. For most businesses, the cash flow improvement outweighs the fee.

**Alternative:** ServiceM8 supports bank transfer payment links alongside card payments. Encouraging clients to pay via bank transfer on larger jobs ($5,000+) eliminates the fee entirely.

### Cost #4: Onboarding Time Isn't Free

ServiceM8's DIY setup model means you're doing the configuration yourself. For a 5-person business, expect:
- 4-8 hours of setup (services, rates, templates, Xero connection)
- 2-4 hours of staff training
- 2-4 weeks until the team is using it fluently

ServiceM8 offers local setup partners (at additional cost) and 24/7 chat/email support on all paid plans. The free plan is self-serve support only.

## Australian-Specific Considerations

### Compliance Forms Built for Australian Trades

This is where ServiceM8 separates from international competitors. The platform includes pre-built digital forms for:

- **CCEW (Certificate of Compliance for Electrical Work):** Required by state electrical safety regulators including Energy Safe Victoria and NSW Fair Trading. ServiceM8's CCEW form captures all required fields — installation details, circuit protection, testing results — and generates a compliant PDF.
- **CES (Certificate of Electrical Safety):** The Western Australian equivalent, with WA-specific fields.
- **Custom safety checklists:** SWMS, site induction forms, risk assessments — all with geo-tagged photo capture and digital signature.

For electrical businesses regulated under state electrical safety acts (Electricity Safety Act 1998 in Victoria, Home Building Act 1989 in NSW, Electrical Safety Act 2002 in Queensland), having compliance documentation built into the job workflow is not just convenient — it's an audit defence. Missing or incomplete CCEW forms carry penalties up to $40,000 for corporations in some states.

### GST Handling

ServiceM8 handles Australian GST natively — line items can be marked as GST-inclusive or GST-exclusive, invoices calculate GST automatically, and the Xero/MYOB sync preserves GST coding. No workarounds needed for BAS reporting.

### STC and Solar Compliance

For solar installers, ServiceM8's custom form builder can be configured for STC (Small-scale Technology Certificate) documentation — capturing system details, panel serial numbers, inverter specifications, and installation dates. This feeds into STC claim processing and Clean Energy Regulator compliance.

However, ServiceM8 does not have native STC lodgement — you'll still need REC Registry access through the Clean Energy Regulator portal. The forms capture the data; the lodgement is separate.
