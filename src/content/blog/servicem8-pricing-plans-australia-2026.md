## ServiceM8 Pricing Plans — 2026

ServiceM8 uses a job-volume-based pricing model. All plans include unlimited users, the full feature set, and all integrations. The only variable is how many jobs you can create per month.

| Plan | Monthly price (AUD) | Jobs per month | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $29/month | Up to 15 | Solo operators, part-time businesses |
| Grow | $99/month | Up to 50 | 1–3 person teams, residential focus |
| Manage | $199/month | Up to 150 | 3–8 person teams, mixed commercial/residential |
| Unlimited | $349/month | Unlimited | Larger teams, high-volume businesses |

All prices are in AUD and exclude GST. Annual billing options may be available — check the ServiceM8 website for current promotions.

## What's Included in All Plans

Every ServiceM8 plan includes:

**Scheduling and dispatch**
- Drag-and-drop job calendar
- GPS tracking for field staff
- Job status updates (On My Way, Start Job, Finish Job)
- Staff availability and job assignment

**Quoting and invoicing**
- Quote templates with line items and pricing
- Convert quotes to jobs in one tap
- Invoice on the spot from a mobile device
- Automated payment reminders

**Compliance and documentation**
- CCEW (Certificate of Compliance for Electrical Work) — pre-built form
- Site forms, safety checklists, photos attached to jobs
- Digital signatures for job completion and compliance forms
- Document storage per job

**Accounting integrations**
- Xero (real-time sync)
- MYOB AccountRight and MYOB Essentials
- QuickBooks Online
- No extra fee for any of these integrations

**Client communication**
- Automated job confirmation and reminder SMS
- Client portal for viewing job history and invoices
- Email invoice delivery with payment link

**Mobile app**
- iOS and Android
- Offline mode (jobs accessible without mobile data)
- Camera integration for photos, digital forms

## Which Plan Is Right for Your Business?

### Starter ($29/month) — Solo or side businesses

If you're doing fewer than 15 jobs per month, Starter covers you. Suitable for:
- Solo electricians doing maintenance-only work
- Part-time operators with a main employer
- Businesses in early startup with low job volume

The limitation: at 15 jobs/month, you'll hit the cap quickly if work picks up. Upgrading is instant, so don't let this stop you starting on Starter.

### Grow ($99/month) — Small teams up to ~3 staff

50 jobs per month covers most 1–3 person residential electrical businesses. If you're doing 10–15 jobs per week, you're at 40–60 per month — borderline between Grow and Manage.

This is the most popular plan for:
- Small electrical teams doing residential maintenance and installations
- Solar installers doing 10–20 systems per month
- Owner-operators with one or two employees

### Manage ($199/month) — 3–8 person teams

150 jobs per month is high volume for a small business — this plan suits businesses with 4–8 field staff running multiple crews simultaneously.

Works well for:
- Multi-crew electrical businesses with commercial and residential work
- Solar installation teams doing volume residential projects
- Mixed electrical/solar businesses with consistent high weekly job flow

### Unlimited ($349/month) — No cap, high-volume operations

If you're regularly exceeding 150 jobs per month, Unlimited removes the constraint. The cost difference between Manage and Unlimited ($150/month) is negligible at this business size.

## The Real Cost Question

The plan price isn't the real question. The real question is: **does the system pay for itself?**

For most electrical businesses, the answer is yes. The typical ROI drivers:

**Faster invoicing.** Businesses switching from end-of-week manual invoicing to same-day mobile invoicing from ServiceM8 consistently report getting paid 7–14 days faster. On $40,000/month revenue, that's meaningful working capital improvement.

**Fewer unbilled hours.** When every job is in the system with time tracking, nothing falls through the cracks. Businesses typically recover 3–8% of previously unbilled time after systematising job management.

**Compliance time.** For CCEW-heavy businesses, having the compliance form built into the job workflow saves 15–30 minutes per job compared to manual paper forms.

At $99/month, recovering one extra invoice per month more than covers the cost.
