## What Is an AI Business Assistant for Tradies?

An AI business assistant is software that performs admin and customer communication tasks automatically — without you needing to be involved in every interaction.

This is different from standard job management software, which typically requires a human to trigger each action. AI assistants operate on logic and automation rules that handle work in the background 24/7.

For trade businesses, the most valuable AI assistant capabilities include:

- **24/7 call answering and lead capture** — responding to enquiries outside business hours
- **Automated job scheduling** — booking jobs directly into your calendar without back-and-forth
- **Instant quote generation** — producing accurate estimates from standardised pricing
- **Client follow-up sequences** — nurturing leads and chasing invoices automatically
- **Review generation** — requesting Google reviews at the right moment after job completion

The businesses seeing the biggest impact are typically solar installation companies and electrical contractors — trades where inquiry volume is high, jobs require detailed scheduling, and customer expectations for fast responses have increased sharply.

## Building Google Reviews on Autopilot

One area where AI automation delivers outsized returns for trade businesses is Google review generation.

Reviews are the single most important factor in local search visibility for solar and electrical businesses. A business with 80+ reviews and a 4.8 average will consistently outrank competitors in Google Maps results — generating organic leads without any advertising spend.

The challenge: most tradies forget to ask, or feel uncomfortable doing it in person. The result is businesses doing excellent work that have fewer than 20 Google reviews, while average competitors with better systems have 150+.

ServiceM8 solves this automatically. When a job is marked complete, a review request SMS or email is sent to the customer at a preset interval — typically 2–3 days after completion when the experience is still fresh.

The message is personal and professional: it thanks the customer, notes the specific job, and includes a direct link to your Google review page. Customers who were happy with the work (which is most of them) complete the review in under a minute.

Businesses implementing this consistently can add 20–40 new reviews per month without any manual effort.
