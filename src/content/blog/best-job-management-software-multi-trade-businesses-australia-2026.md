Most Australian tradies running multi-licence businesses make the same mistake: they pick job management software built for a single trade and hope it stretches.

It doesn't.

If you're a **plumber who also holds a gas fitting licence**, or an **electrician who does data cabling and security systems**, or a **builder who runs a maintenance division**, single-trade software forces you to choose between compliance and simplicity.

Under the **Gas and Electricity (Consumer Safety) Act 2017** and **Plumbing Licensing Regulations 2020 (VIC)**, each licence you hold carries its own documentation requirements, safety checks, and notification obligations. Missing one because your software wasn't designed for it is a fast track to licence suspension.

This comparison breaks down which platforms actually handle multi-trade operations — and which ones make you work around them.

---

## The Multi-Trade Problem Most Software Ignores

A multi-trade business isn't just "two tradie businesses sharing a bank account." It's:

- **Multiple licence classes** with different compliance workflows
- **Mixed job types** on the same day (gas installation + plumbing repair + backflow testing)
- **Shared customers** who don't care which licence you're using — they just want the problem fixed
- **Unified invoicing** across services, not three separate invoices from three separate apps
- **One set of books** for an ATO that doesn't recognise your internal trade boundaries

Generic job management software handles the first two and silently fails on the rest.

## Why Single-Trade Software Fails Multi-Trade Operators

### The compliance gap

Every licence class has different paperwork. A gas fitting job needs isolation certificates, pressure-test records, and notification to the state regulator. A plumbing job needs different forms. An electrical job needs CCEW or COES documentation.

Software built for one trade bakes in that trade's forms. When you try to use it for a different licence, you're either:
- Using the wrong form (compliance risk), or
- Abandoning the software for that job type (wasted money)

ServiceM8's form builder is the differentiator here. You build the exact form your licence requires — once — and it becomes part of the job workflow every time.

### The pricing trap

Tradify, AroFlo, and simPRO all charge **per user**. If you have a plumber, a gas fitter, and an office manager, you're paying for three seats minimum. Add an apprentice and a second crew, and the monthly bill scales linearly with headcount.

ServiceM8 charges **per job volume, not per user**. A 5-person multi-trade crew on ServiceM8 Growing pays the same $79/month as a single-trade crew. That's a $100–300/month saving at the Growing tier alone.

### The customer record problem

Your customers don't think in licence classes. Mrs Smith just knows her hot water system stopped working and she wants it fixed — whether that's plumbing, gas fitting, or both.

Single-trade software stores customer records inside that trade's workflow. When she calls back six months later for a gas appliance service, you're either:
- Hunting through a different app, or
- Re-entering her details into another system

ServiceM8 keeps one customer record with every job, quote, and compliance document attached. One search, one history, one invoice if she wants it.

## How Each Platform Handles Multi-Trade Work

### ServiceM8
**Strengths:** Form builder is genuinely flexible — you can build any compliance form your licence requires. Unlimited users mean adding a new licence class costs nothing extra. Mobile app is excellent for field techs switching between job types. Customer records are unified.

**Weaknesses:** Form builder only includes basic templates on the Free plan; you need Growing ($79/mo) to build your own. Reporting is good but not enterprise-grade.

**Best for:** Multi-trade operators with 2–15 staff who need different compliance workflows in one place.

### Tradify
**Strengths:** Simple interface, fast onboarding, good for quote-to-invoice workflows. Has basic job scheduling and customer management.

**Weaknesses:** Per-user pricing penalises multi-trade crews. Custom forms are limited — you can add fields but not build full compliance certificates from scratch. No native compliance document templates for gas fitting or multi-licence scenarios.

**Best for:** Single-trade businesses with straightforward quote-job-invoice needs.

### AroFlo
**Strengths:** Strong on service management, good mobile app, handles complex job workflows better than Tradify.

**Weaknesses:** Per-user pricing with a 3-user minimum ($240+/month minimum). Compliance forms require configuration. Not as flexible as ServiceM8's form builder for custom licence documentation.

**Best for:** Service-focused trades (cleaning, pest control, maintenance) with 3+ staff.

### simPRO
**Strengths:** Powerful project management, detailed job costing, strong for large commercial jobs with subcontractor chains.

**Weaknesses:** Per-user pricing, high implementation cost ($3,000–$10,000 typical), steep learning curve. Setup takes weeks, not days. Overkill for most multi-trade businesses unless you're running $500k+ commercial projects.

**Best for:** Large enterprises (20+ staff) doing complex commercial solar, electrical, or plumbing with multi-stage billing.
