Running a solar installation business means managing a complex mix of compliance, quoting, scheduling, and documentation — none of which a generic spreadsheet or basic invoicing tool handles well. The right job management software can be the difference between a business that runs smoothly and one that haemorrhages time on admin.

This guide compares the leading platforms on what actually matters for solar businesses in Australia: CEC documentation, solar-specific workflows, integration with Xero, and real pricing for 2026.

---

## What Solar Installers Actually Need From Software

Solar installation has distinct requirements that separate it from general electrical work:

**Multi-day job management.** A commercial solar install isn't done in a day. The software needs to handle multi-day scheduling, track progress across visits, and manage teams across different phases (survey, install, inspection, commissioning).

**CEC and compliance documentation.** CEC accreditation requires specific documentation at installation, including site assessment, design documentation, and grid connection paperwork. Good software builds these into the job workflow — not as attachments, but as forms the installer completes on-site.

**Quoting with product catalog.** Solar quotes are complex — panel model, inverter, battery, mounting hardware, cable, switchboard works. A product catalog with current pricing, with line-item quoting that feeds into an invoice, saves significant quoting time.

**STC and rebate tracking.** Keeping track of which installations have had STCs assigned and which Small-scale Technology Certificates are outstanding is a compliance and cash flow issue. Software that tracks this saves paper-based chase-up.

**Post-installation follow-up.** Warranty claims, monitoring setup, annual check-in — solar businesses with good post-installation workflows generate referrals and recurring revenue.

## Platform Comparison

### ServiceM8 — Best for small-to-medium solar teams

**Pricing:** $29–$349/month (no per-user fee, job volume-based)
**Best for:** 1–10 staff, residential and light commercial solar

ServiceM8 handles the solar workflow through its mobile-first approach. The CEC compliance documentation lives as custom forms attached to jobs — the site assessment, installation checklist, and handover documents are completed in the app on-site. Everything is GPS-stamped and date-stamped automatically.

**Solar-specific strengths:**
- Custom form builder: create your CEC survey, install checklist, and handover document as mobile forms
- Product catalog with solar equipment pricing
- Client portal: customers can access installation documents post-job
- Xero integration: STCs and invoice line items sync in real time
- On-the-spot invoicing from mobile after job completion
- Quote-to-job conversion with full line items

**Limitations:**
- Not purpose-built for large commercial multi-day projects (manageable for most small-medium solar businesses, but simPRO handles complex project management better)
- No dedicated STC tracking module

**Verdict:** The right choice for most Australian solar installation businesses up to about 10 staff. Cost-effective, mobile-first, and handles the compliance documentation requirements through the custom forms system.

---

### simPRO — Best for larger solar operations and commercial projects

**Pricing:** ~$150–$250/user/month (varies by module and contract)
**Best for:** 10+ staff, complex commercial solar projects

simPRO has a dedicated solar module that handles design documentation, CEC compliance workflows, multi-day project management, and estimating. It's the most comprehensive platform for large solar operations.

**Solar-specific strengths:**
- Solar project module with CEC workflow integration
- Advanced estimating for commercial projects with labour and material cost tracking
- Multi-stage project management for large installations
- Strong reporting on project profitability
- CEC documentation and grid connection tracking

**Limitations:**
- Significantly higher cost (per-user pricing, plus implementation fees)
- Steeper learning curve — typically requires 2–4 weeks onboarding
- Overkill for businesses doing fewer than 15–20 systems per month

**Verdict:** Worth the cost for businesses doing consistent commercial solar work with 10+ staff. For smaller operations, the cost and complexity is hard to justify versus ServiceM8.

---

### Tradify — Budget option for smaller operators

**Pricing:** ~$35/user/month
**Best for:** 1–5 staff, simpler workflow needs

Tradify is simpler than ServiceM8 — it handles quotes, jobs, invoicing, and scheduling but with less depth on compliance documentation and custom forms.

**Solar-specific reality:** Tradify lacks a solar-specific compliance forms system. You can attach documents to jobs, but there's no custom form builder for structured CEC documentation. For residential solar with simple documentation needs, it works. For businesses that need structured compliance workflows, it's a limitation.

**Verdict:** Fine for small solar businesses prioritising simplicity over compliance depth. Not ideal if CCEW and CEC documentation are a priority.

---

### AroFlo — Enterprise option for larger businesses

**Pricing:** Custom per-user pricing, typically $100+/user/month
**Best for:** 15+ staff, complex project management

AroFlo's project management and compliance documentation is thorough — it handles multi-stage projects well, has strong asset management, and suits businesses with complex job costing needs. High cost and implementation overhead make it hard to justify for typical solar installation businesses.

---

### Fergus — Mid-tier option

**Pricing:** ~$49/user/month
**Best for:** Small-medium trades businesses

Fergus sits between Tradify and simPRO in sophistication. It handles quoting, scheduling, and invoicing well. Solar documentation support is limited compared to ServiceM8's custom forms system.

## Making the Decision

For **most Australian solar installation businesses** — 1–10 staff, mix of residential and light commercial — **ServiceM8 is the right choice**. The cost saving versus simPRO alone ($350–$600/month for a 3-person team) is substantial, and the CEC documentation needs are met through the custom forms system.
