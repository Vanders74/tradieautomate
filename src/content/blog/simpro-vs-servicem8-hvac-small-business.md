Small HVAC operators get quoted Simpro setup fees and walk away confused. The software does more than they'll ever need, costs significantly more than they expected, and requires weeks of configuration before a single job is in the system.

The real question isn't which software is "better." It's which software is right for your business at its current scale.

Here's the honest breakdown.

---

## Who This Comparison Is For

This article is written for small to mid-sized HVAC businesses: typically 2–5 technicians, focused on residential and light commercial work. The main job types are split-system supply-and-install, ducted system servicing, breakdown and repair callouts, and refrigerant handling compliance work.

If you're running a large mechanical engineering firm doing major commercial HVAC construction with significant subcontractor management and BIM documentation requirements, Simpro is probably the right call. This comparison isn't for you.

---

## Simpro: Where It Wins

Simpro is enterprise-grade field service software. It was built for large, complex operations that need:

**Detailed job costing at project level.** Simpro allows you to cost materials, labour, and subcontractor charges against individual jobs and cost centres with granular precision. For a large mechanical services contractor running multi-month commercial plant installations with dozens of line items, this matters.

**Inventory management.** Full stock control, purchase order management, supplier pricing catalogues, and location-specific inventory tracking. If you're running a large van fleet with significant parts stock or managing a physical warehouse, Simpro's inventory module is comprehensive.

**Multi-branch operations.** Simpro handles businesses operating across multiple locations with separate P&Ls, cost centres, and reporting hierarchies. If you're running offices in Sydney, Melbourne, and Brisbane with separate technician pools and management structures, Simpro scales to that.

**Subcontractor management.** Building and managing subcontractor relationships, tracking their certifications, and issuing purchase orders to them — Simpro handles this well.

**The honest caveat:** All of this comes with complexity. The average Simpro implementation for a business under 10 technicians takes 6–12 weeks, requires dedicated training, and often needs a consultant to configure it correctly. The platform is powerful — but it's built for businesses with the administrative capacity to use it.

## ServiceM8: Where It Wins for Small HVAC

ServiceM8 was designed for small and medium trade businesses doing mobile service work. For a 3-man HVAC operation, here's why it often fits better:

**Speed to productive use.** Most small trade businesses are operational in ServiceM8 within a day. The interface is intuitive for technicians who aren't naturally tech-savvy, and the mobile app works the way tradies actually work — on a phone, in the field, often with gloves on.

**Quote, job, invoice workflow.** The core workflow is tight: create a quote on-site or in the office, convert to a job when approved, track progress through the job, invoice on completion and collect payment via Stripe. For a business doing 10–30 HVAC jobs per month, this covers the majority of your operational needs.

**The Asset Management module.** This is the stand-out feature for HVAC contractors specifically. ServiceM8's Asset Management add-on allows you to log every piece of equipment you service — split-system indoor unit, outdoor unit, control board — against a specific location and client record. You can record:

- Serial numbers and model numbers
- Install date and warranty expiry
- Full service history with photos and notes
- Scheduled maintenance due dates

When a breakdown call comes in for a client you serviced last year, the technician pulls up the job in ServiceM8 and sees the full asset history before they even leave the depot. They know the model, they know what was last done, they know if there's a known issue. This dramatically reduces first-visit resolution time.

**Automated customer communications.** Confirmation texts when a job is booked, reminder texts before the appointment, "I'm on my way" notifications from the technician's app, and follow-up review requests after job completion — all automatic. For a small HVAC business competing on customer experience, this is significant.

**Refrigerant compliance.** Australia's refrigerant handling regulations require documentation of refrigerant recovered, added, and disposed of for each applicable job. ServiceM8 allows this to be captured as part of the job record, creating an auditable trail without additional paperwork.

**Pricing for a 3-man team.** [ServiceM8's pricing](/blog/servicem8-pricing-plans-australia-2026) is based on a monthly subscription with per-staff pricing. For a 3-technician operation, the all-in cost including Asset Management typically runs $150–$250/month AUD. Simpro enterprise pricing for the same team size is typically $350–$600/month, plus the significant implementation cost upfront.

## What Most Small HVAC Businesses Actually Need Day-to-Day

It's worth being specific about the daily workflow for a 3-technician HVAC operation, because software choice should be grounded in the actual tasks you're running:

**Morning:** Office allocates jobs to technicians. Technicians check their ServiceM8 queue, see the day's jobs with addresses, customer notes, and asset history. No morning briefing call required — all the information is in the app.

**On-site:** Technician arrives, updates job status to "In Progress." Pulls up asset history — sees the split-system was last serviced 14 months ago, has a note from the previous tech about a deteriorating capacitor. Carries out the service, captures photos, records refrigerant gas added.

**Completion:** Job completed on-site. Technician fills in service record, attaches photos, customer signs off digitally. Invoice generated automatically. Payment collected via Tap to Pay if the customer is present.

**Back-office:** Office sees all jobs completed, all invoices generated, payments synced to Xero. No re-entry.

This is the workflow ServiceM8 was built for. Simpro does the same thing — but requires more steps, more screens, and more configuration to get there.

## The Transition Cost: Why Businesses Stick with the Wrong Software

One thing this comparison doesn't fully capture: the cost of switching. If you're already on Simpro and have three years of job history, customer records, and asset data in the system — the switching cost is real. Data migration, retraining, the transition period where productivity drops — these are legitimate considerations.

The right time to make this decision is before you commit to either platform. If you're setting up a new business, or evaluating software for the first time, the decision is clean. If you're already embedded in one platform and it's working well enough, the marginal improvement may not justify the switching cost.

For new HVAC businesses or those still on spreadsheets and paper: ServiceM8 is the right starting point. It will handle your operational needs until you hit roughly 10–15 technicians with significant commercial contract complexity. At that scale, revisit the decision.

## The Decision Logic

**Choose ServiceM8 if:**
- Your jobs are primarily residential or light commercial HVAC (split-systems, ducted servicing, breakdowns)
- You want to be operational in days, not months
- Your biggest admin pain points are quoting, scheduling, invoicing, and customer communication
- You want to build a systematic service history database for your equipment base
- You're not managing significant inventory or multi-branch complexity

**Choose Simpro if:**
- You're doing large-scale commercial mechanical construction with project-level job costing
- You manage significant inventory and warehouse operations
- You run multiple branches with separate reporting requirements
- You have the administrative capacity (and budget) to implement and maintain enterprise software

For most 3-man HVAC businesses reading this article, ServiceM8 is the right call. Not because Simpro is bad — it's exceptional at what it does — but because it's built for a scale and complexity that a 3-man team doesn't have yet.

Start with the right tool for where you are now. When you're at 15 technicians running commercial projects, revisit.

## FAQ

### Can ServiceM8 handle refrigerant logging for Australian compliance?

Yes. ServiceM8 allows you to capture refrigerant gas quantities (recovered, added, disposed of) as custom fields within the job record. This creates a per-job record of refrigerant handling that satisfies Australian refrigerant handling documentation requirements. For businesses with ARC accreditation, this digital record replaces manual paper log books.

### Does Simpro have a free trial?

Simpro offers a demo rather than a self-serve free trial. You'll be walked through the platform with a Simpro consultant. This reflects the onboarding complexity — Simpro needs to be configured for your business before it's meaningful to trial. ServiceM8, by contrast, offers a self-service 14-day free trial you can set up in 15 minutes.

### How do ServiceM8 and Simpro compare for preventive maintenance scheduling?

Both platforms handle scheduled maintenance. In ServiceM8, you can set recurring jobs against specific assets — a "6-month service" for a split-system that automatically generates a job every 6 months. Simpro's planned maintenance module is more sophisticated for large commercial plant (multiple assets, complex schedules, compliance window tracking), but for residential and light commercial HVAC, ServiceM8's recurring job functionality is sufficient.

### Can I migrate data from Simpro to ServiceM8?

Yes, though it requires some work. ServiceM8 can import client and job data from CSV files. Simpro data can be exported to CSV. The migration isn't turnkey — some manual cleaning and re-formatting is typically needed — but it's manageable for a business with a few hundred clients. ServiceM8's support team can assist with migration planning.

The Asset Management add-on is strongly recommended for any HVAC business — it's the feature that most directly addresses HVAC-specific workflow requirements.
