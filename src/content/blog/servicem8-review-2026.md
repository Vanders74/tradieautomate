## Features We Love

### 1. Compliance-Ready Job Forms

For solar installers and electricians operating under strict regulatory requirements, ServiceM8's digital forms are exceptional. You can build custom compliance checklists, inspection reports, and certificate of compliance templates that field staff complete on-site via the app.

The regulatory landscape for Australian electricians is demanding: under **NSW Fair Trading's CCEW framework** (*Home Building Act 1989* and *Electricity (Consumer Safety) Act 2004*), a Certificate of Compliance Electrical Work must be lodged within 7 days of job completion — with penalties up to $22,000 per offence for non-compliance. In Victoria, **Energy Safe Victoria requires CES lodgement within 30 days** under the *Electricity Safety Act 1998 (Vic)*. Nationally, Clean Energy Regulator audits under the *Renewable Energy (Electricity) Act 2000* require complete installation records including geo-tagged photos and test results.

ServiceM8's digital job forms are built to capture all of this at the point of work — not in a Friday afternoon paperwork scramble.

Photos attach directly to job records — not floating in someone's camera roll. Every site visit is timestamped and auditable. When Fair Trading NSW, Energy Safe Victoria, or the Clean Energy Regulator comes knocking, your documentation is already organised.

Working with trades businesses preparing for CER audits, I've seen this shift firsthand: companies that previously spent two days pulling together paper records for a spot audit now do it in under two hours — because everything is tied to the job record automatically.

### 2. Automatic Invoice Generation

When a job is marked complete in the field, an invoice can be generated and emailed to the client automatically — without the office touching it. Payment links, card processing, and Xero sync come standard.

The cash flow impact is real. A solar installation business running 20 jobs per month at an average of $8,000 per job — if they cut their average payment cycle from 25 days to 9 days, that's roughly **$260,000 more working capital** moving through the business at any given time. Not an edge case. A pattern that repeats across businesses that implement ServiceM8's invoicing workflow properly.

### 3. Unlimited Staff Logins

Unlike most competitors, ServiceM8 doesn't charge per user. You can add every apprentice, subcontractor, and admin staff member without worrying about escalating per-seat costs. This matters when you're scaling from 5 to 20 staff.

### 4. GPS and Arrival Tracking

Live GPS tracking for field staff with automated customer notifications ("Your technician is 10 minutes away") reduces inbound calls, improves customer satisfaction, and lets the office monitor job progress without micromanaging.

### 5. iOS and Android Apps

The mobile apps are genuinely polished — one of the strongest in the category. Field staff can quote, invoice, take photos, capture signatures, and complete compliance forms without a paper form in sight.

## Limitations

**No native project management for large jobs.** ServiceM8 is optimised for single-visit or multi-stage jobs, but it's not designed for complex, multi-month construction projects with cost tracking across dozens of cost codes. If you're doing large commercial solar farms, you may need to supplement with project management tooling.

**iOS-first experience.** While Android works, the iOS app has historically received features and polish first. Android users report a minor but noticeable gap in experience.

**Add-on costs add up.** The base plan is affordable, but enabling advanced features like route optimisation, job costing, or certain integrations pushes the monthly cost higher than the headline price suggests. Calculate total cost carefully before committing.

**Limited custom reporting.** Out-of-the-box reporting is functional but basic. Businesses that need deep operational analytics will find the built-in reports limiting and may need to export data to a separate BI tool.

**Not ideal for enterprise scale.** ServiceM8 is optimised for businesses with 1–50 field staff. Beyond that, platforms like simPRO with dedicated project management modules may be more appropriate.
