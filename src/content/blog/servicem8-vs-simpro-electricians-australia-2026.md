ServiceM8 and simPRO dominate the job management conversation for Australian electrical contractors. They're both capable platforms — but they're built for different business sizes and workflows.

Before comparing features, the compliance stakes need stating. Under the **Home Building Act 1989**, **NSW Fair Trading** requires electrical contractors to lodge a CCEW within 7 days of completing prescribed work — with penalties of $22,000 for individuals and $110,000 for corporations. The **Clean Energy Regulator (CER)** audits solar installations under the **Renewable Energy (Electricity) Act 2000**, requiring complete documentation demonstrating compliance with **AS/NZS 5033** and **AS/NZS 5139**. Your job management software either supports this workflow — or it becomes your audit failure point.

Choosing the wrong one is an expensive mistake. This comparison is written specifically for electrical contractors: CCEW compliance, electrician-specific workflows, and realistic pricing for trade business sizes.

## The Core Difference

**ServiceM8** was built for mobile-first small trade businesses. The whole philosophy is: get field technicians quoting, scheduling, complying, and invoicing from their phone, with minimum back-office complexity.

**simPRO** was built for project-based trade operations. The philosophy is comprehensive management — estimating, multi-phase projects, detailed job costing, asset management, preventative maintenance scheduling. More power, more complexity, more cost.

For electricians: if your work is mostly residential and light commercial installation and maintenance, you need ServiceM8's workflow efficiency. If you're managing large commercial projects with multiple crews over multiple days and need granular cost tracking, you need simPRO's project depth.

## CCEW and Compliance — Critical for Electricians

### ServiceM8

ServiceM8 includes a **pre-built CCEW form** in the job workflow:

1. Job is completed in the field
2. Electrician opens the CCEW form from the job in the ServiceM8 app
3. Completes the certificate fields digitally
4. Customer signs on the technician's phone screen
5. Certificate is automatically saved to the job and emailed to the customer

No paper, no separate PDF process, no administrative follow-up. The CCEW is part of the natural job completion workflow.

For state compliance in other states (CES in VIC, CEC in SA, EWC in WA), ServiceM8's custom form builder lets you create structured digital compliance forms for any requirement.

### simPRO

simPRO does not have a native CCEW form. It supports:
- Document attachment to jobs (upload a completed PDF)
- Custom field creation on job records
- Form templates for structured data capture

The compliance workflow is less integrated. For regular CCEW completion, you'd typically maintain a separate PDF process and attach the completed document — functional, but more manual than ServiceM8's native integration.

For larger commercial electrical projects where compliance involves complex multi-page documentation, simPRO's document management is more robust.

## Mobile Experience

### ServiceM8
ServiceM8 is iOS-first and the mobile app reflects this. It's polished, fast, and designed for field technicians who may not be technical:
- Clean job list and calendar
- "On My Way" tap-to-notify: sends automated SMS with ETA link to customer
- In-app form completion, photo capture, signature collection
- Offline mode (access jobs without mobile data)
- Invoice on the spot, accept payment via Stripe

Field staff feedback on ServiceM8 is consistently positive — the interface is straightforward enough that a new technician can be operational on the first day.

### simPRO
simPRO's mobile app (simPRO Mobile) has improved significantly in recent years. It handles job viewing, time entry, progress tracking, and document access. It's functional but less polished than ServiceM8 — the interface reflects the system's complexity, which is appropriate for its enterprise use case but can feel heavy for field technicians doing straightforward residential work.

## Which Electrical Businesses Should Choose Each

### Choose ServiceM8:
- 1–15 staff electrical business
- Residential, light commercial, and maintenance work
- Regular CCEW compliance requirements (NSW)
- Want fast setup and minimal IT involvement
- On Xero — want seamless real-time integration
- Cost-sensitive (fair price for the feature set)

### Choose simPRO:
- 10+ staff with dedicated admin and management layer
- Regular large commercial electrical projects (fitouts, data centres, industrial)
- Need granular multi-phase project costing and tracking
- Significant preventative maintenance contracts with asset management
- Can invest 2–4 weeks in implementation and ongoing system management
- Budget allows for the significantly higher cost

### The middle ground:
For electrical businesses growing from 5 to 15+ staff and starting to take on larger commercial projects, there can be a genuine transition point. The practical approach: run ServiceM8 until the commercial project complexity clearly exceeds what it handles, then evaluate simPRO at that stage. Don't pay for simPRO's complexity before you need it.
