simPRO is Australia's most recognised enterprise trade management platform. It's used by medium-to-large electrical, HVAC, plumbing, and mechanical services businesses — and it's frequently the first alternative considered when a growing trade business feels they've outgrown simpler tools.

But "more powerful" doesn't always mean "right for your business." This review gives you an honest picture of what simPRO does well, where it falls short, and who genuinely benefits from its complexity and cost.

---

## What simPRO Is Built For

simPRO was designed for project-based trade management at scale. The core use cases it handles best:

**Multi-phase commercial projects** — simPRO's project module handles multi-day, multi-crew commercial jobs with stage tracking, progress billing, and detailed cost capture at every phase. A $500,000 electrical fitout with 6 crews across 12 weeks is where simPRO shines.

**Preventative maintenance contracts** — simPRO has a dedicated preventative maintenance module that manages asset registers, triggers scheduled maintenance jobs, and tracks service history. For businesses with long-term maintenance agreements, this is genuinely valuable.

**Detailed job costing and profitability** — simPRO tracks labour cost, material cost, and margin at the job level in detail. Businesses with thin margins on commercial work use this to identify which jobs are profitable and which aren't.

**Multi-trade or multi-division businesses** — businesses operating across electrical, data, security, and HVAC under one company can manage all divisions through simPRO.

## What simPRO Does Well

### Project management
For complex commercial trade work, simPRO's project management is the strongest in the Australian market. Multi-stage projects, progress claims, retentions, variation tracking, and detailed cost reporting are all handled natively.

### Asset and preventative maintenance
The asset management module is comprehensive. Create asset registers for client sites, schedule periodic maintenance, track service history, and generate compliance reports. For facilities management and maintenance-heavy businesses, this is hard to replicate elsewhere.

### Estimating
simPRO's estimating module handles large commercial quotes with bill-of-materials, labour rates, supplier pricing, and margin calculation. For a 50-page commercial electrical tender, this level of estimating depth is genuinely useful.

### Reporting
Advanced reporting on project profitability, technician productivity, revenue by client, and WIP (work in progress) gives management-level visibility that simpler platforms don't offer.

---

## Where simPRO Falls Short

### Cost
The per-user pricing model is expensive for growing teams. A 10-person business pays $1,500–$2,500/month — and the cost grows linearly with every hire. ServiceM8's Unlimited plan at $349/month covers any team size.

### CCEW compliance (NSW electricians)
simPRO does not have a pre-built CCEW form. NSW electrical businesses must manage Certificate of Compliance documentation separately — typically a PDF process alongside simPRO. For businesses doing regular residential electrical work, this is a meaningful workflow gap.

### Implementation time
Getting simPRO operational typically takes 2–6 weeks including data setup, configuration, and staff training. Some businesses hire implementation consultants. If you need to be running in days, simPRO isn't that platform.

### Mobile experience
simPRO Mobile has improved but still reflects the system's enterprise complexity. Field technicians with less technical confidence often find the interface heavier than ServiceM8. The learning curve in the field is real.

### Flexibility
The 12-month contract and structured pricing means limited ability to scale down if the business changes. ServiceM8's monthly billing with instant plan switching is more flexible.

## Who Should Use simPRO

**Good fit:**
- 10+ staff trade businesses doing consistent commercial project work
- Businesses with ongoing preventative maintenance contracts and asset management needs
- Operations where detailed multi-phase project costing is essential
- Companies with dedicated admin staff to manage the platform

**Not a good fit:**
- Residential or light commercial electrical and solar businesses under 10 staff
- Businesses that need to be up and running within days
- Cost-sensitive operations where $750–$2,500/month is a material constraint
- NSW electrical businesses needing built-in CCEW compliance

## The Verdict

simPRO is a legitimate enterprise platform for large Australian trade businesses. The project management, asset management, and reporting depth is genuinely best-in-class for complex commercial operations.

But it's too expensive, too complex, and too slow to implement for the majority of Australian electrical and solar businesses. If you're running residential and light commercial work with a team under 10, the $600–$1,000/month you'd save on ServiceM8 is real money — and ServiceM8 handles your actual workflow better.

Evaluate simPRO seriously when: you're consistently running commercial projects over $200,000, you have preventative maintenance contracts that need systematic management, and you have admin staff who can own the platform. Until then, there are better-value options.
