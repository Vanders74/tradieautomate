Most tradies choose their job management software first and bolt accounting on later.

That's backwards.

Your job management platform and your accounting software are the two halves of your operational backbone. The integration between them determines whether your BAS takes two hours or two weeks, whether your accountant calls you with questions or with surprises, and whether you can see job-level profitability or just a monthly bank balance.

In Australia, two stacks dominate trade business: **ServiceM8 + Xero** and **Tradify + MYOB**. Both are legitimate. But they're built for different business types, and picking the wrong combination costs money and admin hours.

This comparison breaks down the real differences — pricing, compliance workflows, BAS preparation, and what a 5-person electrical or solar crew actually pays.

## The Stack No One Talks About

When you read software reviews, they almost always evaluate one product at a time. "ServiceM8 review" or "Tradify review."

But nobody runs just one piece of software. Your stack is the combination of field app + accounting + payroll + payments. The integration quality between those tools matters more than any individual feature.

For Australian tradies, the two dominant stacks are:

1. **ServiceM8 + Xero** — Australian-built field app + Australian-built accounting
2. **Tradify + MYOB** — NZ-built field app + Australian-built accounting

Both stacks work. The question is which one works *better* for *your* business.

## Pricing: The Gap Is Bigger Than You Think

### ServiceM8 + Xero

| Component | Plan | Monthly cost (AUD, incl GST) |
|---|---|---|
| ServiceM8 Growing | $79/mo | $79 |
| Xero (Essentials) | $42/mo | $42 |
| Xero (Standard) | $62/mo | $62 |
| **Combined (5-person crew)** | | **$79–141** |

ServiceM8 Growing includes unlimited users, 150 jobs/month, 300 SMS, and the form builder. Xero Essentials handles GST, BAS, and basic reporting. Xero Standard adds multi-currency and payroll for up to 5 staff.

**Key point:** ServiceM8 doesn't charge per user. A 5-person crew pays the same $79 as a 2-person crew. That's the single biggest cost advantage.

### Tradify + MYOB

| Component | Plan | Monthly cost (AUD, ex GST) |
|---|---|---|
| Tradify Pro (5 users) | $52/user | $260+ |
| MYOB Essentials | $30/mo | $33 (incl GST) |
| MYOB AccountRight | $77/mo | $85 (incl GST) |
| **Combined (5-person crew)** | | **$293+** |

Tradify charges per user. Five staff on Pro is $260/month excluding GST. Add MYOB and you're at $293+ including GST — nearly double the ServiceM8 + Xero stack.

**The exception:** A sole trader on Tradify Starter ($49) + MYOB Essentials ($33) pays $82/month. That's comparable to ServiceM8 Growing ($79) + Xero Essentials ($42). But the ServiceM8 stack gives you unlimited users and stronger compliance forms at the same price point.

## Compliance: Where ServiceM8 + Xero Wins

### CCEW and state licensing

Under the **Gas and Electricity (Consumer Safety) Act 2017 (NSW)**, electrical work must be documented with a CCEW lodged in BCNSW eCert within 7 days. The **Electricity Safety Act 1998 (VIC)** requires a Certificate of Electrical Safety (COES) for prescribed work.

ServiceM8's form builder lets you create CCEW and COES templates that field staff complete on-site. The form auto-populates from job data, captures photos, and stores with the job record. You can export the completed form as a PDF and lodge it manually, or set up a Zapier workflow to push it to BCNSW.

Tradify supports custom fields and attachments, but its form builder is less flexible. Building a full CCEW workflow in Tradify requires workarounds that most tradies don't have time to maintain.

### GST and BAS

Xero's Australian tax engine is built for GST, BAS, and single-touch payroll. When ServiceM8 pushes an invoice to Xero, the GST status, income account, and contact details are already mapped. Your accountant opens Xero and the data is there.

MYOB handles GST and BAS correctly, but its job-level project accounting is less intuitive. Tradify invoices sync to MYOB, but pulling job-level P&L by trade type requires manual coding that most bookkeepers charge extra to fix.

## Mobile App: Field Techs Notice the Difference

### ServiceM8 app
- Offline-first design (works at roof level with no signal)
- Forms complete on-site with photo capture
- GPS job clock and route optimisation
- Client signatures on the spot
- Push notifications for new jobs

### Tradify app
- Online-only in most workflows (some offline caching)
- Basic job viewing and status updates
- Good for simple scheduling but weaker on field data capture
- Limited offline form completion

If your techs spend most of their day on job sites with patchy reception — which describes most electrical, solar, and plumbing work — ServiceM8's offline app is a genuine productivity advantage.

## The Accountant Factor

Here's something most software reviews ignore: **your accountant's opinion matters**.

Australian accountants overwhelmingly use Xero. The ATO's Single Touch Payroll and Single Touch Enrolment systems integrate directly with Xero. Most BAS agents and small-business accountants are Xero-certified and MYOB-aware but Xero-fluent.

When you hand your accountant a Xero file, they know where to look. When you hand them a MYOB file, they can work with it — but Xero's reporting ecosystem is deeper, its add-on marketplace is larger, and its bank feeds are more reliable with Australian institutions.

If you're choosing between stacks and your accountant says "I prefer Xero," that's not a minor preference. It's a time-and-money decision.

## What This Means for Your Business

If you're a **compliance-heavy trade** — electrical, solar, HVAC, plumbing with gas fitting — ServiceM8 + Xero is the stronger stack. Lower cost, better compliance, better mobile app, and an accounting ecosystem your accountant already knows.

If you're a **simple trade** with no compliance documentation — residential cleaning, basic maintenance — Tradify + MYOB works fine. You're paying more for features you don't need, but the integration is solid.

The middle ground — multi-trade operators with some compliance requirements — is where the decision matters most. ServiceM8 + Xero scales better as you add licences, staff, and job complexity. Tradify + MYOB hits a ceiling around 5–8 users before the per-user pricing and limited forms start creating friction.

## FAQ

### Is ServiceM8 + Xero cheaper than Tradify + MYOB for Australian tradies?

Yes, in almost every scenario. ServiceM8 charges per job volume, not per user, so a 5-person crew on ServiceM8 Growing + Xero pays roughly $79–109/month combined. The same crew on Tradify Pro + MYOB pays $260+ per month for Tradify alone, plus MYOB Essentials or AccountRight. The gap widens as you hire. The exception is a sole trader on Tradify's unlimited plan plus MYOB Essentials — that can be cheaper than ServiceM8 + Xero at the very smallest scale.

### Does ServiceM8 integrate better with Xero than Tradify integrates with MYOB?

Both integrations are mature, but they work differently. ServiceM8 + Xero syncs invoices, contacts, and payment status in real time with minimal configuration. Tradify + MYOB also syncs invoices and contacts, but MYOB's mapping requires more setup and some Tradify features (like progress invoicing) don't map cleanly to MYOB's project accounting. For businesses that live in their accounting software, ServiceM8 + Xero is smoother.

### Which stack is better for BAS and tax time?

ServiceM8 + Xero. Xero's Australian tax engine is purpose-built for GST, BAS, and single-touch payroll. MYOB is compliant but its project-level reporting is less intuitive for trade businesses that need to see gross margin by job. ServiceM8 pushes job-level data (materials, labour, markups) into Xero, so your accountant can slice P&L by job type, trade, or customer without leaving the spreadsheet. Tradify + MYOB can do this but requires more manual mapping.

### Can I use ServiceM8 with MYOB, or Tradify with Xero?

Yes — both platforms integrate with both accounting packages. ServiceM8 has native MYOB integration, and Tradify has native Xero integration. The combinations above (ServiceM8 + Xero, Tradify + MYOB) are simply the most common pairings among Australian trade businesses, and they happen to be the strongest integrations in each ecosystem.
