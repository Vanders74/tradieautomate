Most "best job management software" lists are written for established businesses with 5+ staff, established workflows, and a clear idea of what they need. They recommend platforms that cost $150/month and take three days to configure.

If you're three months in, still quoting on paper, and trying to work out whether to spend $29 or $149 on software — that advice is useless.

This comparison is different. We evaluated ServiceM8, Tradify, AroFlo, and Fergus specifically for **new trade businesses** — sole traders and micro-businesses (1–2 operators) in their first 12 months. The criteria that matter to you are different: setup time, first-year cost, room to grow, and whether you can test before you commit.

## The New Business Criteria No One Else Uses

Established businesses judge software on features. New businesses judge it on:

- **Can I test it for free?** — You haven't built a reputation yet, so every dollar counts.
- **How long to set up?** — You're already working 60-hour weeks. Software that takes three days to configure is software you won't use.
- **Does it grow with me?** — You don't want to switch platforms when you hire your first apprentice.
- **Can I learn it myself?** — You don't have an office manager. If the software needs a consultant, it's the wrong software.

We weighted these criteria above features like job costing or project management — because you don't need those yet. You need quoting, invoicing, and scheduling that doesn't require a manual.

## Tradify: Simple, But You Pay Per Person

Tradify's strength is simplicity. The interface is clean, the onboarding is fast, and most sole traders are productive within a day.

**The catch:** Tradify charges per user. A sole trader on Starter pays $49/month. Add an apprentice and you're at $98. Add an office manager and you're at $147. By the time you have 5 staff, you're paying $245/month — and that's before MYOB integration costs.

Tradify does offer a 14-day free trial, which is enough to evaluate the platform. But there's no free plan for ongoing use, so you're committing from day one if you want to keep your data after the trial.

**Best for:** New businesses that know they'll stay small (1–2 operators) and prioritise simplicity over compliance features.

---

## AroFlo: Powerful, But Pricey for Day One

AroFlo is a capable platform with strong job management and a good mobile app. It handles complex workflows better than Tradify and has more flexibility for service-focused trades (cleaning, pest control, maintenance).

**The problem for new businesses:** AroFlo's pricing starts at $99/month with a 3-user minimum. That's $297/month minimum commitment before you've landed your first recurring client. No free plan, no single-user plan.

AroFlo also has a steeper learning curve. Setup takes 2–4 hours, and the configuration options can overwhelm new operators who just want to quote and invoice.

**Best for:** New businesses with 3+ staff from day one and a service model that justifies the cost.

---

## Fergus: Good for Trades, Limited Scope

Fergus is an Australian-built job management platform designed specifically for trades. It handles quoting, invoicing, and scheduling well, and its mobile app is solid.

**The limitation:** Fergus is narrower than ServiceM8 or Tradify. Its form builder is less flexible, its reporting is basic, and it doesn't scale as cleanly as you add staff or services. The pricing starts at $49/month (per user), which is competitive, but the per-user model punishes growth.

Fergus also lacks MYOB integration — Xero only. If your accountant uses MYOB, that's a problem.

**Best for:** New electrical or plumbing businesses that want an Australian-built platform and are certain they'll stay on Xero.

## What to Do in Your First Week

If you're launching this week:

1. **Sign up for ServiceM8 Free** — no credit card, 30 jobs, full feature access.
2. **Build one compliance form** — if your trade requires it (CCEW, COES, gas certificate).
3. **Create your three most common job types** — the ones you'll actually do in month one.
4. **Connect Xero or MYOB** — the integration takes 10 minutes and it's one less thing to do later.
5. **Run five test jobs** — quote, schedule, complete, invoice, sync to accounting. If it feels clunky after five jobs, try Tradify for a week.

The goal is not to optimise your software. The goal is to stop doing quotes on paper and admin in your head by the end of month one.
